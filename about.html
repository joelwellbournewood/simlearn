About SimLearn

SimLearn is a growing collection of interactive simulations for exploring ideas from science and nature — from emergent flocking behavior to ecology, physics, and complex systems. Each simulation is grounded in an established model or theory and built to be played with, not just read about.

Every simulation on this site is a self-contained module. The site itself is just the catalog and the frame around them, so new simulations can be added over time without touching what already works.
