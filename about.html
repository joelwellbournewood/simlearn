About SimLearn

SimLearn is a collection of interactive simulations. Each one runs an established scientific model live in your browser, and each one is built to be adjusted while it runs.

The point is to make behaviour that is hard to describe in words easy to see. A flock has no leader. A traffic jam can appear on an empty road. A city can segregate itself even when nobody wants it to. Those claims are easier to believe once you have moved the sliders yourself and watched it happen.

Everything runs on your own machine. There is no account, no tracking and nothing to install. New simulations are added one at a time.
