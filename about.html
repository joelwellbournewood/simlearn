What SimLearn is all about

Curiosity and fun keep us with a problem far longer than duty does, and the time we spend experimenting because you want to see what happens is usually the time that teaches you the most.

SimLearn is a collection of interactive simulations, designed for play and learning, Based on scientific and mathematical models of real dynamics, they are made to be tested, challenged, and broken. We find the limits of our understanding through play.

Everything runs on your own machine. There is no account, no tracking, and nothing to install. New simulations will be added as they become available.
