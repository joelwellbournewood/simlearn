Predator & Prey

Agent-based version of the Lotka–Volterra predation model. Prey (green) breed and roam; predators (red) chase, eat prey to gain energy, and starve without food. Watch the population chart below settle into the classic offset boom-bust cycle: prey rise → predators rise → prey crash → predators crash → repeat.
