Predator & Prey

Agent-based Lotka–Volterra predation with real steering: prey (green) actively flee any predator within their vision radius and graze energy otherwise; predators (red) actively hunt and steer toward the nearest visible prey. Both use spatial-grid neighbor search so hundreds of agents run smoothly. Prey growth is capped by a carrying capacity, so the boom-bust population cycle below repeats indefinitely instead of exploding or crashing for good.

Prey Predators

Click the canvas to drop food — nearby prey get an energy boost, which can rescue a crashing population or fuel a bigger predator boom later. Try pushing predator efficiency high and watch predators overhunt, crash, then let prey rebound.
